Glossary & Methodology
Latency / Score: Raw time (µs/op) or Normalized Score (pts). Benchmark: Simple (flat dict), Complex (mixed), Nested (deep).
Evolution Path: A Pareto-style frontier mapping Time (Speed) against RAM (Efficiency). The path traces the architectural maturity of the project.
Throughput (MB/s): Theoretical maximum data rate during O(1) streaming operations, derived from a 45MB benchmark payload.
